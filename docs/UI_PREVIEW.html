Round report
What actually changed in the app.
The gradient is android:windowBackground, so every screen gets it with no per-layout work and there is no flash of a flat colour at launch. Every screen root is now @android:color/transparent — that is what lets the cards be translucent. Cards gained a 20 dp radius, a translucent fill and a 1 dp lighter rim (glass_stroke); elevation went to 0 because a hard drop shadow under frosted glass looks wrong. The toolbar, the bottom bar, the five bottom sheets and the dialogs all use the same treatment, with the sheets rounded at the top only.

Second pass — what was weak and what changed.
The gradient was too pale. The original three stops were all within a few points of white, so there was almost nothing to see through a translucent card — and a pane you cannot see through is a flat panel with a faint tint. The stops are now genuinely coloured and the blobs went from 20% to 30% alpha.
A third blob, centred. With only two corner blobs the middle of a tall screen had no colour in it, so a card scrolled to the middle had nothing behind it to refract — exactly the moment the glass stopped looking like glass.
The bars are now 97% opaque, and the edge fades are gone. They looked good in this mockup and were a real bug on the device: content scrolls UNDER those bars, and at 85% the scrolled text stayed legible through them and collided with the toolbar title — two layers of text over each other. An app bar is not a card: a card has only the gradient behind it, a bar has the whole scrolling app.
Corner radius 20dp → 24dp, and the suggestion card now carries a coloured rim, which is what separates "the answer" from the seven figures around it.

Not shown here: the map and the route screen. They use the same gradient, the same card style and the same chrome — this is the design system, not a screenshot of the build. A real Android render still has to be checked on your phone.

Phase 12 — the round report (third panel). Reached from the History tab, which now carries a header card showing the round in progress at a glance. Each round card answers the question the day list could not: 65 stores · 4 days, broken into Day 1 · 1 Sep — 23 stores and so on. Tapping a day row reveals the store codes done that day. Rounds come back newest first; days come back oldest first, because that is the order they happened in.
